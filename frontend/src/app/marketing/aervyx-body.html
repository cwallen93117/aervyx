Soaring forecast Under Development

XC Skies and Skylight —
without the subscription.

Six weather models, physics-based thermal forecasts, interactive soundings, and wind barbs — integrated directly into your task map.

Six Weather Models

HRRR 3km, NAM 3km, RAP 13km, GFS 25km, NAM 12km, and NBM 2.5km. Switch models in one click to cross-reference forecasts. Hourly updates from NOAA via Herbie — no API keys, no subscriptions.

Thermal Forecast (W*)

Deardorff convective velocity derived from sensible heat flux, boundary layer height, and virtual temperature. No ad-hoc cloud penalties — the physics does the work. Fixed color scales for day-to-day consistency.

Soaring Quality Index

A composite 0–10 score blending thermal strength, boundary layer height, cloud cover, and surface wind. One glance tells you if the day is soarable — without juggling five different overlays.

Interactive Soundings

Click anywhere on the map for a Skew-T diagram with temperature, dewpoint, dry adiabats, and pressure levels from 1000–200 hPa. Multi-model comparison at any point.

Wind Barbs at Altitude

Meteorological wind barbs at 10m, 850 hPa, 700 hPa, and 500 hPa. Color-coded by speed with density-aware spacing. Proper convention — direction shows where wind blows from.

Animated Time Scrub

Drag through the full forecast timeline or hit play to animate. Day headers, hour ticks, and a run-date badge so you always know which model cycle you're viewing.

Tracking infrastructure

Coverage where the
thermals take you.

Two complementary technologies ensure no pilot goes dark — even in remote mountain terrain without cellular.

📶

Cellular Tracking

The Aervyx pilot app streams GPS over 4G/LTE. Any smartphone, zero extra hardware, background-optimised battery usage.

  • 2–5 second update interval over cellular
  • Works with any modern smartphone
  • Background tracking — active while flying
  • Auto-fallback to mesh when signal drops
  • Battery-optimised position reporting
🔗

Meshtastic Mesh Networking

Off-the-shelf Meshtastic LoRa devices form a self-healing ground relay network. The Aervyx app relays heard mesh positions over HTTPS, with private MQTT available for fixed gateways.

  • LoRa range: 10–50 km per node in open terrain
  • Self-healing mesh — nodes relay for each other
  • Works with off-the-shelf Meshtastic hardware
  • App relay plus optional fixed MQTT gateways
  • Ideal for remote ridge and alpine sites
Meshtastic mesh tracking

Off-grid coverage for
every flying site.

Meshtastic LoRa devices form a self-healing radio mesh that works where cell towers don't. Affordable, solar-powered, and purpose-built for remote terrain.

🏆

Competition Safety

Real-time pilot positions for safety directors and retrieve teams. Mesh SOS alerting is under development. Under Development

🪂

Free Flight Clubs

Install a solar-powered node at your club's launch and LZ. Every member with a Meshtastic device is automatically tracked — no subscriptions, no cell plans, no recurring costs.

⛰️

Remote Sites

Mountain launches, desert thermals, coastal ridges with no coverage — LoRa reaches 10-50 km per hop in open terrain. The mesh fills gaps that cellular simply can't.

💲

Affordable Hardware

Complete Meshtastic nodes cost $20-40. Solar-powered and weatherproof options exist for permanent outdoor installation. No monthly fees, no SIM cards, no data plans.

🚗

Retrieve Vehicles

Mount a node on each retrieve vehicle. Drivers stay visible on the tracking map and relay positions for pilots overhead — turning every car into a mobile mesh repeater.

📍

Landing Zone Routers

Leave a fixed node at the LZ before you launch. It acts as a relay point for your flight — capturing your positions and forwarding them through the mesh to the cloud.

How it works

Each pilot carries a small LoRa radio that broadcasts GPS positions. Nearby nodes relay the signal hop-by-hop until it reaches a paired Aervyx phone app or a fixed MQTT gateway, which forwards positions to the Aervyx cloud. The mesh is self-healing — add or remove nodes and coverage adapts automatically.

GAP-aligned formulas

Uses the GAP2021 formula family — the same model as FAI Cat 1 events. Configure nominal distance, time, launch, and minimum distance per task.

Provisional → Official

Live provisional scores update as tracks arrive. Once the window closes, the director confirms official results — full audit log retained throughout.

SHA-256 evidence chain

Every IGC upload is fingerprinted and stored immutably. Original files are never mutated — full traceability for protests and federation records.

Fixed Total Validity (FTV)

Pilots' best results up to a configured validity percentage count toward the series total. Protects against one bad day ruining a season — standard for multi-event series and FAI Cat 2 events.

Multi-Day Events

Per-task day quality, aggregate overall standings, and cross-day FTV calculations. Directors can score any subset of tasks and finalize day-by-day as the event progresses.

Public Results Portal

Shareable public URL for every event. Live provisional results visible throughout the task — no login required for spectators. Official results published after the director confirms.
